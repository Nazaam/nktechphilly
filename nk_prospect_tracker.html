Stage 1
Open - Make them comfortable
2 min
Opening line
"Thanks for taking a few minutes. Before I tell you anything about what we do, I'd love to understand your situation first - that way I can tell you honestly whether we're even a fit. Is that okay?"
Disarms them. Positions you as consultative. Gives you permission to ask questions.
Stage 2
Diagnose - Let them talk 80%
8-10 min
Q1 - Current state
"How are you currently handling IT and network support when something goes wrong?"
Q2 - Pain probe (most important)
"Has there been a time recently where your internet or phones going down actually cost you time or money?"
Their words become your closing language. Don't suggest the pain - let them describe it.
Q3 - Frequency
"How often does that kind of thing happen?"
Q4 - Impact
"When it goes down - what does that look like for your team? What stops working?"
Q5 - Frustration
"Is there anything about your current setup you wish was more reliable or faster to fix?"
Stage 3
Position - Their pain to your solution
3-4 min
Formula
"Based on what you just described - [their words] - what we do for businesses like yours is [solution]. That usually means [their outcome]."
The line that closes
"It sounds like what you really need is someone you can just call - one person who knows your setup and actually fixes it. That's exactly what we are."
Stage 4
Close - Get a decision
2-3 min
Soft close
"Based on what you've told me, I think the Standard plan at $449/month would be the right fit - month-to-month, no commitment. I can send a one-page agreement today. Would that work?"
Never leave without a next step
"I'll send this today - when can I follow up? Thursday work?"
Objections

"What specifically is on your mind? Is it the price, timing, or something else? I'd rather address it now than have you sitting with a question I could answer in 30 seconds."
"It's too expensive" ▼
"What does an hour of downtime cost when your team can't work? Most clients find the plan pays for itself after one avoided outage."
"We already have someone" ▼
"Is that person monitoring proactively, or do you call when something breaks? Most businesses don't realize they have the reactive version until something expensive happens."
"No budget right now" ▼
"If your internet went down tomorrow and your team couldn't work for 3 hours, what would that cost? Our Basic plan is $249/month. One bad afternoon is usually more than that."
"Need to talk to my partner" ▼
"When would be good for a quick three-way call? I can answer any questions - usually takes 10 minutes."
